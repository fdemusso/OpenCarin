# Changes log — OSM against the disc

2026-10-01. Read-only study: no disc image is written, nothing is run on a unit. Every number below is
reproduced by the script named next to it; the full text reports are written to `dataset/report_*.txt`
(`dataset/` is git-ignored and regenerated by the scripts).

## What each dataset is and how to regenerate it

| File (`dataset/`) | What | Made by |
|---|---|---|
| `disc_<disc>_<area>_segments.csv.gz` etc. | every segment of the street tiles covering the area, all fields of the 32-byte record, nodes, S10, S11/S12, tile headers, house-number scheme | `01_extract_disc.py` (about 10 s per area) |
| `osm_<area>_now.json.gz` | Overpass `way[highway]` with geometry and tags, state of 2026-10-01 | `02_fetch_osm.py` |
| `osm_<area>_now_rel.json.gz` | `type=restriction` relations (members). **Only Bari and `x_slovenia` were downloaded** | `02_fetch_osm.py` |
| `match_<disc>_<area>_now.csv.gz` | disc segment -> OSM way | `03_match.py` |
| `sample_21708_400_*.csv.gz` | 400 random street tiles of the whole disc (192,779 segments) | `08_sample_disc.py` |
| `hn_index_<disc>.json.gz` | tile -> its `0x04` house-number block | `01_extract_disc.py` |

Size of `dataset/`: about 20 MB. Areas: `bari_modugno` (16.74–16.95 E, 40.98–41.20 N), `rural_a14`,
`altamura` (Puglia) and six one-tile areas elsewhere in Europe (`x_*`) chosen from the random sample for
their rare values. Disc segments inside the Puglia boxes: 42,518 (21708) and 51,145 (21734).
Matched with OSM (one way holds >= 70% of the samples within 15 m and 30°): 22,461 + 6,438 + 3,914 of
21708's Puglia segments (the rest is footways, tracks and roads OSM maps differently or not at all).

## What could not be done (read this first)

- **No 2015 snapshot.** `overpass-api.de` answered with `Dispatcher_Client::request_read_and_idx::timeout`
  for every attic request (`[date:"2015-07-21T00:00:00Z"]`) over about two hours; the mirrors
  (`kumi.systems`, `private.coffee`, `osm.jp`) did not answer, `overpass.openstreetmap.fr` needs a whitelist,
  `maps.mail.ru` gave 504. Geofabrik's dated 2015 files did not answer either. **All OSM comparisons are
  against today's OSM**, so they include real changes of the last 11 years. The tool is ready
  (`02_fetch_osm.py --snapshot 2015`); the comparison "2015 against disc" is not done.
- Turn restrictions (`type=restriction`) were obtained for Bari only; the S10 tests against them cover that area.
- Everything about meaning is read from data. **Nothing was run on a unit.**

## Verdicts per field of the worklist

Legend: **explained** = holds on a large sample and could have failed; **hint** = clear association, not a rule;
**unknown**. "Generator" = what a disc built from OSM has to do for the field.

### 1. Form of way 7 (`+0x0B & 0x0F == 7`) — hint; the name of the property is unknown

- Values seen: 712 segments on 21708 (Puglia); **0 of 51,145 on 21734**.
- Disc against disc (`09_disc_vs_disc.py`, 17,973 pairs with identical shape): form 7 on 21708 becomes 11 (202) or
  12 (187) on 21734; class, direction, junction and subtype do not change on them (0 of 374 Puglia pairs); speed changes on 4 and `+0x1D` on 16.
  So form 7 is a compiler-version artefact of the 14q4 build, not a property of the road.
- OSM (today, Bari, 341 matched form-7 segments): highway primary 17.9% (0.9% of the rest), secondary 30.2% (9.2%),
  trunk 7.9% (1.3%); `oneway=yes` 88.9% (35.9%); `lanes=2` 43.4% (9.0%); residential 6.5% (37.1%).
  The disc says two-way (direction bits 0 on all 712) for roads OSM maps as one-way. Their stored direction is not
  related to the OSM direction (along: 158 against, 145 with).
- S10: 295 / 341 have S10 entries with flag 2/3 on the owner (item 4).
- Contradicts: the PR #31 hint that owners usually have a dead end at one node: **768 / 785** owners of flag 2/3 entries
  on 21708 (976 / 995 on 21734) have no dead end at either node. Form 7 is also not required for flag 2/3:
  it is absent on 21734 and flag 2/3 owners there have form 12 (486) or 11 (475).
- Generator: write form 11/12 (what 21734 does). Form 7 is not needed.

### 2. `+0x10` bit 7, `+0x1D` bits 4-6, `+0x18 & 0x10`

- **`+0x10` bit 7 — hint (strong): placeholder class.** Always on class 6 / subtype 1 (3,013 of 3,021 in the random sample).
  Absent in Puglia (0 of 42,518). In Slovenia the whole minor road network of 21708 (1,907 segments) is class 6 / sub 1 /
  bit 7; on 21734 the same roads are class 4 (333 segments), 5 (215), 6 without the bit (324), and 747 keep it. Of the
  384 paired segments with the bit on 21708, 28 drop only the bit, 17 become class 4, 10 class 5. In Czechia it stays
  (1,394 / 1,402 segments on both discs). Today's OSM tags on them: tracks, driveways, `noname=yes`, `access=permit`.
  Reading: where the source gave no functional class the compiler writes class 6 with this bit; it is a data-coverage
  marker. Not derivable from OSM. Generator: 0 (we have a class from `highway`).
  RR firmware: the edge field fed by it (`+0x1D` of the edge) selects between a default and edge byte `+0x16` at
  `rpmod` `0x03cffc`–`0x03d03c`, a cost computation; the layout of that edge was not traced.
- **`+0x1D` bits 4-6 — unknown.** 528 / 192,779 sampled segments (values 6: 446, 3: 76, 1: 3, 2: 2, 5: 1), all on
  class 5 or 6, speed category 2 or 5, found in Germany, France and the Netherlands but 0 in Puglia and Czechia.
  In the Duisburg tile they sit on parking aisles and one one-way tertiary road (`service=parking_aisle` 28.6% against 0.8%).
  They are spatially clustered, not tied to a tag: no OSM tag separates them (best: `access=no` 9.5%, 6 of 63).
  Generator: 0.
- **`+0x18` = 4 — hint: unpaved / rural track.** 1,051 matched segments: `highway=track` 34.4% (1.3% of the rest), length >= 200 m
  43.5% (8.9%), `surface=unpaved/gravel/ground/dirt`, `tracktype` grade3/4, `oneway=yes` 2.9% (37.2%). Only 362 of the 777 OSM
  tracks have it, so it is not "all tracks". On the disc it is almost only class 5 (1,811 of 1,953). 21734 adds 43 and
  removes 13 on identical shapes. Generator: 4 on class 5/6 tracks and paths with an unpaved surface is a reasonable
  default; unverified rule.
- **`+0x18 == 0x10` — unknown.** 8 segments in Puglia (none matched), 68 in the random sample, classes 0-3 and on main roads.

### 3. `+0x11 >> 4` (high nibble) and junction 3 / 4 — hint

- Values: 2 on 192,669 / 192,779 sampled segments; 1 on 65 and 4 on 45, all with junction type 3 (96) or 4 (14).
  Junction 3 is absent in Puglia; it occurs in Germany (Duisburg, Gennep) and elsewhere.
- OSM, junction 3, high nibble 4 (24 matched): `highway` tertiary 8, residential 11, path 2, footway 2, living_street 1; `motor_vehicle`
  allowed on 21 / 24. High nibble 1 (10 matched): service 7, path 2, tertiary 1; no car access tag needed, all 10
  are parking aisles / paths. This is the firmware rule (`can_traverse`: junction 3/4 closed unless the high nibble is 4)
  and it agrees with OSM.
- Junction 3 comes in clusters: 34 / 44 (Duisburg), 28 / 33 (Gennep), 6 / 10 (Solingen) have another junction 3 segment within 60 m.
  In Gennep the nibble-4 segments are the old town (Grosser Markt, Lohstätte, An der Münze: pedestrian and 30 km/h paved streets).
  So junction 3 reads as "inside a pedestrian / shared-space area"; nibble 4 = open to cars, 1 = not.
- Not decidable from OSM tags alone (no tag separates them: `surface=paving_stones` 33% against 2%). Generator: leave junction 3 unused
  until a polygon rule (`highway=pedestrian` areas, `living_street` zones) is tested on a unit.

### 4. S10 flags 2 and 3, self entries of flags 0 / 1

Disc data (Puglia, 21708 / 21734): flag 0 and 1 entries into another segment 1,039 + 1,037 / 978 + 975; flags 2/3 self entries
483 + 481 / 623 + 617, into another segment 258 + 254 / 310 + 302; flag 0/1 self entries 10 + 5 on both discs.

- **Flag 0 / 1 into another segment = forbidden turns: confirmed against OSM restrictions** (Bari, `04_form7_s10.py`): 34.4% of the owners
  (684) lie on a way that is in an OSM `type=restriction` relation, against 4.4% of the rest (`only_straight_on` 108, `no_left_turn` 75,
  `no_u_turn` 47, `only_right_turn` 32, `only_left_turn` 24, `no_right_turn` 22). Also: `highway=*_link` and `maxspeed=40` junctions.
- **Self entries of flag 0 / 1 (U-turn ban?) — unknown.** Only 7 owners matched; 1 is on a `no_left_turn` way. Too few to say.
  `no_u_turn` relations were not used in this test (they join two ways, not a way and itself).
- **Flags 2 / 3 — unknown, hypothesis "no through traffic" at a dead end refuted.** 531 matched owners:
  - 768 / 785 owners (21708) have no dead end at either node (976 / 995 on 21734); median length 73 m (all segments 45–49 m).
  - OSM restriction relations: 9.8% of the owners (419) against 5.4% of the rest, i.e. almost no signal (`only_straight_on` 22, `no_u_turn` 12,
    `no_left_turn` 12).
  - Association with OSM tags: `highway=primary` 14.8% (0.9%), `lanes=2` 33.9% (9.1%), `oneway=yes` 83.1% (35.8%), `maxspeed=60` 4.1% (0.2%),
    `highway=residential` 7.9% (37.2%): main, multi-lane, mostly one-way roads.
  - Among segments OSM maps as one-way but the disc maps as two-way (direction bits 0): 279 / 1,774 have flag 2/3 entries, against
    69 / 10,356 of the real two-way ones. The pattern (flag 2 and 3 both, only 2, only 3) does not follow the OSM direction.
  - Owners are mid-network (768 / 785 not at a dead end), class 1–4, 21708: form 7 for 558 of 785, 21734: forms 12 and 11.
  Generator: write no flag 2/3 entries (the disc routes without them in the 85% of such segments that have none).

### 5. Node flags (S5 / S6 `+6`)

Puglia, 21708: 45,185 nodes (`05_nodes.py`).

- **Bits 15–14, bit 13, bit 12 — explained** (already known): level reached; bit 13 set on every S6 edge node (5,867 / 5,867) and never on S5;
  bit 12 set on every S5 node (39,318) and on 189 S6 nodes (79 with in-tile degree 2, 64 with degree 1; 187 on street-only level).
  Why those 189 edge nodes have bit 12 — unknown.
- **Nibble N (bits 11–8) — explained for 0, 1, 5; hint for 2 and 4.** N = 1: S5 nodes of degree 1 (6,420 / 6,420). N = 5: S5 nodes of degree 2
  (4,938 of 4,986) and S6 edge nodes with one in-tile segment (5,687 of 5,765; such a node has degree 2 across the border). N = 0:
  degree >= 3 (22,279 of 22,399 interior nodes of degree 3, 5,368 of 5,378 of degree 4).
  RR firmware reads `+6 & 7 == 1` (dead end) and `3 - (bits 7-6)` (level).
  N = 4 (48 S5 nodes of degree 2): the two segments differ in no attribute more often than for N = 5 (19 / 48 name changes against 1,170 / 4,938);
  all 48 have `+0x1C` != 0x10 (bridge / tunnel bits) but today's OSM calls 29 / 30 matched ones ground level: **unknown**.
  N = 2 (40 nodes, degree 3–4): slip-road and main-road junctions (12 of 29 interior nodes have a slip-form segment against 562 / 21,372 for N = 0):
  **unknown**.
- **Low byte — explained:** 1 and 2 on the two nodes of a crossing without junction (101 and 95; 89 and 88 have N = 5).
- Disc against disc (15,361 nodes at the same position): 208 change flags, almost all N = 0 <-> 5 (degree changed: `0xd000 -> 0xd500` 60,
  `0xd100 -> 0xd500` 48) or level (`0xd000 -> 0x9000` 19). Generator: compute from the graph (degree, level, section, crossings).

### 6. Speed category (`+0x0A` bits 0–4) and built-up bit — hint

- OSM `maxspeed` is a weak predictor in Puglia: of 1,472 segments with `maxspeed=50`, category 11 holds 1,147 (78%); of 1,135 with 30:
  11 holds 561 and 5 holds 511; 90 -> 22 (242 / 273); 130 -> 31 (49 / 52); 70 -> 16 (61 / 118); 60 -> 16 (32 / 47).
- Majority-vote predictor, trained on even BLOCK_IDs and tested on odd (7,052 segments): class alone 5,441 correct (77%); class + built-up
  5,706 (81%); class + form 6,099 (86%); class + form + built-up 6,164 (87%); class + form + built-up + direction 6,267 (89%);
  adding `highway` or `maxspeed` lowers it (6,067 and 6,105). **The category is a default per road type, not the limit.**
- Without `maxspeed` (18,711 segments) the value follows the class (class 4: 11 for 5,478 of 5,767 residential; class 6 footway/pedestrian: 2;
  class 5: 5; class 0 trunk: 22; motorway: 31 or 26).
- Built-up (`+0x0A` bit 7): density test: the bit is set on 184 / 2,040 segments with few neighbours within 150 m, 2,661 / 4,563 with 12–25,
  6,389 / 6,419 with 50 or more; `highway=residential` 47.6% (10.3%), `lit=yes` 20.6% (4.3%), `postal_code` 14.5% (1.3%). Density rule only;
  not tested as a rule on held-out tiles.
- Generator: class by `highway`, category from a table keyed on (class, form), built-up from neighbourhood density.

### 7. Signposts (S11) and TMC (S12)

- **Signposts: explained as "motorway and trunk junction exits and ramps carrying `destination`"** (hint): 341 segments, 726 entries; 180 / 666 matched
  segments on OSM `*_link` ways have signposts against 83 / 21,795 elsewhere. `highway=trunk_link` 41.1% (0.9%), `motorway_link` 11.0% (0.2%).
  Text against OSM `destination`: of the 234 entries on ways with a `destination` tag the sign text is found there for 146 (62%). Class 0–4.
  Generator: build signposts from `destination` / `destination:ref` of ramps; text agreement 62%.
- **TMC: unknown source.** 4,319 of 42,518 segments (classes 0–3: 95% of class 0 and 85% of 1), `highway=primary/secondary/tertiary`, `lanes=2`, asphalt, 90 km/h; direction
  codes 6 / 7 / 8 (5,613 / 5,612 / 3,229); the flag word takes the values 0x1045, 0x2069, 0x3093, 0x0021, 0x8213 (3,907 / 2,674 / 2,613 / 1,468 / 1,177).
  Location codes come from the TMC location table, not from OSM. Generator: omit.

### 8. S7 shape-point flag, S1 / S3, header words

- **S7 flag `+4` — hint (strong, internal): marks shape points of structures.** Flag 1 occurs only on segments with `+0x1C` = 0x16 (199 of 206),
  flag 2 only on 0x18 (97 of 109); no flag on `+0x1C` = 0x10 (0 of 32,054). 0x18 against OSM: `bridge=yes` 15.9% (0.1%), `layer=1` 22.8% (0.2%), i.e. bridges /
  flyovers (OSM `bridge=yes`: 62 of the 390 segments with bit 3 set; the rest are ground-level or carry only `layer=1`); 0x16 against OSM: `tunnel=yes` 14 of 369 (3.8%), `toll=yes` 13.6%, `maxspeed=130` 13.6%,
  `maxheight=default` 10.6%, `lit=no` 28.2%, motorway 16% and trunk 20%: tunnels, underpasses and covered stretches of the main roads, not only tunnels. Whether
  the flag marks the portal points: not tested (needs positions along the way).
- **`+0x1D` bit 0 — explained: the segment has house numbers.** The `0x04` record of the segment has at least one side range: 16,548 / 16,548 segments with the bit set,
  0 / 25,970 without (21708); 21,468 / 21,468 and 0 / 29,677 on 21734. This is also why the bit changes between releases (130 -> 131 on 605 segments of 21708's
  pairs). Class 6 and the other classes alike. Generator: set it when the segment gets a number range.
- Header words: the per-tile words that match counts are explained (word 13 = segment count, 15 = S5 nodes, 17 = S6 nodes, 19 = shape points, 25 = S10 entries,
  27 = S11, 29 = S12 on 108 / 108 tiles). The words "24–26" named in `03-road-network.md` were not found as such in the 116-byte prolog; the words that still vary
  and equal none of our counts are 34–45, 49, 51 and 53 (indices in 16-bit words; the list also holds the section descriptor words). Not resolved.

### 9. What else varies

`10_bytes.py` section A lists every offset: in Puglia only `+0x0A`, `+0x0B`, `+0x0C`, `+0x0D`, `+0x0E`, `+0x0F`, `+0x10` (11 values), `+0x11` (5 values), `+0x18` (6), `+0x1C` (8),
`+0x1D` (20) vary; `+0x19` is always 0. `+0x1C` other than 0x10, 0x16, 0x18: 0x1e (16), 0x19 (9), 0x1d (8), 0x1c/0x1b/0x1a (a handful) — combinations of bridge and tunnel bits, not explained.

## Questions only a unit (or the RR firmware) can answer

1. Does form 7 or flag 2/3 on a segment change the route (time cost or turn legality) on a unit? Try the same road with and without.
2. What does the RR cost routine at `rpmod` `0x03cffc` do with edge `+0x1D` (`+0x10` bit 7) and `+0x16`? Trace which edge layout `$s2` is there.
3. Is `+0x18 == 4` read as "unpaved"? Is it used for route cost? (`sub_01fd80` edge `+0x1F` only reads the `0x10` bit.)
4. What do junction 3 with high nibble 1 / 4 do in the planner (we only have the can_traverse rule)?
5. Does the unit draw a bridge differently when `+0x1C` is 0x18, and the S7 flag points mark where?
6. Node flag N = 4 and N = 2: what do they change?
7. Are U-turn bans (flag 0 / 1 self entries) enforced?
